CONCEPTUAL LEARNING

Think beyond the product.

Use concepts to explain why your design decisions matter.

01

Form

What does the solution look and feel like? How does its structure affect its purpose?

02

Function

What is the solution designed to do, and how effectively does it do it?

03

Innovation

What makes the idea useful, different or better for the intended user?

04

Sustainability

How can the solution reduce negative impacts and remain useful over time?

CRITERION B

Developing Ideas

Turn research into possible solutions and make justified choices.

Strand 1 — Develop design specifications

Task-specific clarification

Create measurable criteria that your final solution should meet, based on research and user needs.

Student evidence

📌 Add your design specification table and success criteria.

Strand 2 — Develop alternative ideas

Task-specific clarification

Generate several different ideas and communicate them clearly using sketches, labels, annotations or models.

Student evidence

📌 Add sketches, mind maps, annotations or alternative concepts.

Strand 3 — Present the chosen design

Task-specific clarification

Present the chosen idea in enough detail for another person to understand how it will work.

Student evidence

📌 Add your final annotated design, dimensions, materials and key features.

Strand 4 — Develop planning drawings

Task-specific clarification

Produce accurate planning drawings or diagrams that communicate how the solution can be made.

CRITERION C

Creating the Solution

Plan, build and document the solution carefully.

Strand 1 — Construct a logical plan

Task-specific clarification

Create a sequence of steps, identify resources and include safety or time considerations where relevant.

Student evidence

📌 Add your production plan, timeline, materials list or workflow.

Strand 2 — Demonstrate technical skills

Task-specific clarification

Show appropriate skills while creating the solution. Explain important techniques and decisions.

Student evidence

📌 Add process photos, screenshots, code, making techniques or skill demonstrations.

Strand 3 — Follow the plan

Task-specific clarification

Use your plan effectively while making the solution and document changes when they are necessary.

Student evidence

📌 Add progress documentation and explain any changes from the original plan.

Strand 4 — Demonstrate quality

Task-specific clarification

Show that the finished solution works and is presented with appropriate quality for the target user.

Student evidence

📌 Add photos/video link of your completed product or working prototype.

Making reflection

CRITERION D

Evaluating

Test the solution, use evidence and think about the next version.

Strand 1 — Design testing methods

Task-specific clarification

Explain how you will test the solution fairly and what evidence will show whether it meets the specifications.

Student evidence

📌 Add your testing plan, test users, questions and success measures.

Strand 2 — Evaluate against specifications

Task-specific clarification

Use test results to judge each design specification and support conclusions with evidence.

Student evidence

📌 Add test results, user feedback, data and your evaluation table.

Strand 3 — Explain impact

Task-specific clarification

Explain how successfully the solution addresses the original problem and affects the target user or context.

Student evidence

📌 Add your impact analysis and evidence from users or the community.

Strand 4 — Suggest improvements

Task-specific clarification

Identify realistic improvements and explain how they would make the solution more effective.

Student evidence
